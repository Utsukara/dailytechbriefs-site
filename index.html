Daily Tech Briefs

Short technology news: what happened and why it matters.

Daily Tech Briefs is a YouTube channel (@dailytechbriefs) that publishes short videos about technology news. Each story is drawn from established news organizations, and the sources are listed in every video's description. Narration and graphics are computer-generated.

The uploader application

This site is also the home page of the private application its operator uses to upload finished videos to two YouTube channels the operator owns: Daily Tech Briefs and AllStreamClips. The application is used only by the operator, with the operator's own Google account. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.
